The range

Every pump has a VME product code. Quote it on your enquiry, or give us your own part number and we will match it.

Spare parts

Keep existing pumps running

We hold spare parts at our Birmingham distribution centre, so replacements can go out on their own without waiting for a full pump build.

Order by code or by your own part number. Each component has a VME code, such as BR-001 for a bearing. If your company uses its own part numbers, we keep a cross-reference so the right part ships every time.

We also supply the smaller items used in assembly and maintenance: O-rings (OR-001), gaskets (GAS-001), fasteners (FST-001), paint (PNT-001) and labels and packaging (LAB-001).

Our sites

One company, two sites

Manchester builds. Birmingham stocks and distributes. Finished pumps move from one to the other by internal transfer, so stock is close to where customers need it.

Manchester

Manufacturing

Where VME pumps are designed and built.

  • Raw materials store: castings, impellers, shafts, bearings, seals and motors
  • Assembly to a bill of materials for each model
  • Finished pumps for direct dispatch or transfer to Birmingham

Birmingham

Distribution centre

No manufacturing happens here. Everything arrives from Manchester and goes out to customers.

  • Finished pumps ready to ship
  • Spare parts store for replacement and maintenance orders
  • Dispatch to UK customers and export shipments
